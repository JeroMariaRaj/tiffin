Traditional Kalyana Sapadu & Wedding Banquets

The grandest 32-course authentic banana leaf feasts for memorable wedding ceremonies.

Sacred Banqueting

The Royal Grandeur of South Indian Weddings

In Tamil and South Indian culture, a wedding is forever remembered by the warmth and richness of its Kalyana Sapadu.

For over three decades, TASTE OF SOUTH has had the privilege of orchestrating wedding feasts for prestigious families across Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, and Bengaluru. Our wedding division provides complete end-to-end culinary hospitality, beginning with the auspicious pre-wedding Vratham breakfast, extending to the grand Muhurtham lunch feast, and concluding with a lavish evening reception buffet.

Our master cooks arrive on-site with massive copper urulis, seasoned soapstone vessels, and hand-selected raw materials. Everything is cooked fresh on location using wood-pressed oils and pure A2 cow ghee. Our disciplined service team wears immaculate silk veshthis and delivers every course with traditional reverence.
