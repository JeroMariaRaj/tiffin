Terms of Dining & Catering Service

Our commitment to authentic South Indian culinary excellence, dining etiquette, and booking guidelines.

1. Reservations & Dining Policy

Table reservations for general dining are held for 15 minutes past the reserved time. Due to high morning tiffin demand on weekends, we kindly ask patrons to arrive punctually.

2. Catering Agreements & Advance Deposits

Wedding banquet and corporate catering bookings require a 25% advance confirmation deposit to secure dates and kitchen team deployment. Final headcount adjustments may be made up to 48 hours prior to the event.

3. Pure Vegetarian Guarantee

TASTE OF SOUTH is strictly 100% vegetarian. All sweets, roasts, and dishes are cooked in pure A2 cow ghee, cold-pressed vegetable oils, and certified clean ingredients.
