Express Takeaway & Tiffin Delivery

Enjoy piping hot Taste of South flavors in your home or on the move.

Express Counter

Hot, Crisp & Perfectly Packed for Your Journey

We solve the age-old problem of takeaway dosas turning soggy: our proprietary breathable banana-leaf packaging keeps roasts crunchy and idlis fluffy.

Whether you are on your morning commute to work, taking breakfast home to loved ones on Sunday morning, or catching an early train from Chennai Central, our dedicated express takeaway wing is engineered for rapid, immaculate service.

Hot items and cold accompaniments are strictly packaged in separate insulated compartments. Sambar and chutneys are poured into heat-sealed leak-proof containers, and our signature degree filter coffee is dispensed into thermal double-walled beverage flasks.

Takeaway Packaging Innovations

Steam-Vented Dosa Sleeves

12-Minute Express Guarantee

Pre-order via phone or web form; your parcel is packaged fresh and ready upon your arrival.

Zero-Leak Sambar Seal

Hermetically heat-sealed soup containers guaranteed to withstand car and bike journeys.

Thermal Coffee Flasks

Insulated 500ml and 1000ml flasks keeping degree filter coffee piping hot for up to 3 hours.
