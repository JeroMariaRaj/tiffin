Culinary Insights

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our stone-ground batters, pure A2 ghee, and satvik preparation.

Taste of South is strictly 100% pure vegetarian with zero meat, fish, eggs, or animal gelatin anywhere in our premises. We cook exclusively with farm-sourced 100% pure A2 cow ghee, cold-pressed sesame (gingelly) oil, and unrefined groundnut oil. We never use palm oil or commercial shortening.

Yes! Our signature Idlis, Thatte Idli, and Ragi Dosas are naturally gluten-free and slow-fermented. For Jain guests, we offer dedicated onion-free, garlic-free sambars and freshly grated coconut chutneys prepared in separate sacred cooking vessels upon request.

Our batter is stone-ground twice every single day using traditional granite wet grinders with premium urad dal from Tirunelveli and aged parboiled rice. The batter is allowed 12 hours of natural slow fermentation without chemical rising agents, baking soda, or artificial preservatives.

Our coffee is crafted with an authentic 80:20 master blend of shade-grown Chikmagalur Arabica peaberry and roasted chicory. Slow-dripped through brass gravity filters, it is combined with thick, unadulterated high-fat dairy milk and served frothing hot in traditional brass davarah-tumblers.

Yes, our kitchen captains gladly tailor any dish. For children and elders with delicate palates, we serve mild butter dosas, non-spicy vegetable kurmas, and cooling curd rice tempered gently with roasted mustard and curry leaves without green chillies.
