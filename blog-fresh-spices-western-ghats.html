The Spice Odyssey: Sourcing Western Ghats Ingredients

By Chef Sadasivam • Apr 09, 2026 • 5 Min Read

"In South Indian cuisine, spice is never a blunt instrument used to burn the tongue. It is a symphony of essential volatile oils orchestrated to ignite appetite and stimulate digestion."

When guests ask why our pepper rasam carries an unforgettable citrusy warmth or why our gun powder podi has such a lingering nutty finish, our answer always leads back to the mountains.

Commercial restaurants purchase pre-ground spice powders in bulk bags from commodity distributors. These powders have often lost their volatile terpenes months before reaching the pan. At TASTE OF SOUTH, we source whole, unadulterated spices directly from certified organic family plantations across the Western Ghats mountain range.

1. Tellicherry Extra Bold Black Peppercorns

Sourced from the slopes of northern Kerala, Tellicherry peppercorns are left on the vine until plump and mature before hand-picking and sun-drying. They contain up to 4.5% piperine, delivering a rich fruity warmth that coats the throat without sharp stinging irritation.

2. Salem Erode Golden Turmeric

We procure unpolished mother rhizomes from smallholder cooperatives in Erode, Tamil Nadu. Boasting a high curcumin concentration of over 5.2%, it imparts a radiant sunshine hue and earthy aroma to our sambars and vegetable kootus.

3. Idukki Green Cardamom & Guntur S9 Chillies

Our sweet Payasams and Mysore Paks feature freshly cracked cardamom pods from high-altitude estates in Idukki, Kerala. For heat, we balance the intense crimson color of Byadagi chillies with the smoky pungency of Andhra Guntur S9 chillies.

Every batch of spice is dry-roasted over gentle low embers in small 5-kilogram quantities twice a week, and freshly pounded in stone mortars right in our Mylapore kitchen.
