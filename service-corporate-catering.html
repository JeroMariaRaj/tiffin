Corporate Catering & Executive Tiffins

Punctual, hygienic, authentic vegetarian meal boxes for leading IT & corporate teams.

Corporate Solutions

Fueling Innovation with Pure Southern Flavors

Elevate your board meetings, quarterly conferences, client townhalls, and training workshops with South India's most dependable gourmet catering partner.

TASTE OF SOUTH delivers hundreds of executive tiffin and lunch boxes daily to tech parks and headquarters across Chennai. Our corporate meals are designed specifically to be wholesome, energizing, and non-drowsy—cooked with wood-pressed oils and pure desi cow ghee without excess heavy greases or artificial additives.

Every order is dispatched in premium, 100% biodegradable areca palm leaf containers with airtight tamper-evident sealing, accompanied by stainless steel cutleries and thermal flasks keeping filter coffee piping hot.
