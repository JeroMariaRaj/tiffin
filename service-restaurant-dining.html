Traditional Restaurant Dining

The authentic Mylapore dining hall with aromatic banana leaf service.

Service Overview

A Heritage Dining Sanctuary in Mylapore

Dining at TASTE OF SOUTH is more than a meal; it is an unhurried cultural immersion into the warmth, sounds, and aromas of South Indian culinary life.

From the moment you step through our teakwood portal, you are greeted with the fragrance of fresh jasmine, the rhythmic metallic clink of coffee davarahs, and the gentle hiss of seasoned cast iron tawas. We seat guests on hand-carved Chettinad benches and solid rosewood tables.

Every afternoon, our lunch session transforms the hall into a grand temple feast. Fresh green banana leaves are laid before each guest, splashed with pure water, and layered with 14 hot delicacies served directly from smoking stainless-steel buckets with infinite generosity.

Dining Features & Highlights

Live Iron Tawa Station

Choose between our breezy sun-dappled courtyard veranda or temperature-controlled halls.
